# ADR-0002: No Device Owner

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** not recorded (before 2026-11-22)
- **Related:** ADR-0001

## Context

Device Owner is an Android role with full control of a device. On Quest only Meta partners (HMS) can become Device Owner.

## Decision

Work without Device Owner. Notice that the player left the game and bring them back.

## Consequences

- We can't block the Meta button.
- **Checked by:** no automatic check. It is a limit of the platform.

## Alternatives considered

Not recorded.
