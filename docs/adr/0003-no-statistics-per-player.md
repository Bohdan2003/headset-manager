# ADR-0003: No statistics per player

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** not recorded (before 2026-11-22)

## Context

The headset does not know who is wearing it.

## Decision

Count statistics by headset and by game, never by player.

## Consequences

- Later we may link a session to a booking by hand.
- **Checked by:** no automatic check. `reviewer` looks for it in the diff.

## Alternatives considered

Not recorded.
